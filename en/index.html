Cook iT – Meal Planner

Help and contact · Deutsch

With Cook iT you plan breakfast, lunch and dinner for the week, collect meals and drinks and always have the matching shopping list with you.

Contact: Questions, bugs or wishes are welcome by email to wiensnikolas@gmail.com.

How do I share my plan with others?

In the meal plan, tap the cloud at the top and then “Share household”. Invited people need their own Apple Account. Afterwards you all see and edit the same meals, the same plan and the same shopping list.

Why isn’t my data syncing?

Syncing works through iCloud. Check that you are signed in with your Apple Account on the device and that iCloud is turned on for Cook iT. Without iCloud, all data stays on the device only.

How does “Recipe from image” work?

In the meals list, tap the sparkle at the top and choose a screenshot of a recipe or a photo of your food. If the recipe is longer than one screen, select all the screenshots in order (up to six). Cook iT turns them into a draft that you check and save. All of this happens entirely on your device.

For a photo of your food, Cook iT recognizes the dish and writes a complete recipe with ingredients, instructions and matching servings. The servings follow from the estimated calories, about 650 kcal per serving; if your hint names a number of people (“for 4 people”), that number is used. If you write “vegan” or “vegetarian” in the hint, the draft is labeled accordingly.

Suggestions for photos need Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later). With iOS 27, Cook iT looks at the photo itself and recognizes dishes much more accurately. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, Cook iT reads the recipe text from screenshots.

Which languages does Cook iT speak?

Cook iT is available in 17 languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Dutch, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese and Korean. The app follows the language of your iPhone. To use a different language for Cook iT only, open Settings › Apps › Cook iT › Language. Recipes from images and the nutrition estimate understand ingredients in your app language and in English.

How are the nutrition values calculated?

Cook iT estimates calories, protein, carbs and fat per serving from the list of ingredients, and for fat also omega-3, omega-6 and omega-9 fatty acids. Amounts such as “500 g”, “2 tbsp” or “1 can” are converted into grams; for ingredients without an amount, the app assumes a typical amount. Under “Check ingredients” you see every assumption and can change foods and amounts or enter the values entirely yourself. In the meals list and while planning the week you can sort by calories, protein, carbs, fat or omega fatty acids and show only vegetarian or vegan meals – you set this when editing a meal under “Diet”. The nutrition values are estimates and do not replace nutritional advice.

Source of the nutrition data: Max Rubner-Institut (2025): Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel (BLS), version 4.0 – German Nutrient Database, blsdb.de, DOI 10.25826/Data20251217-134202-0, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Cook iT uses a rounded excerpt of it (energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat and omega-3, omega-6 and omega-9 fatty acids per 100 g). Omega-9 is the sum of the n-9 fatty acids, mainly oleic acid; where the BLS gives no value for a fatty acid, Cook iT derives it from the totals. Piece weights and default amounts are our own estimates and do not come from the BLS. Food names in languages other than German are our own translations.
