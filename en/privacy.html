Privacy

Cook iT – Meal Planner · Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Deutsch

In short: Cook iT has no ads, no analytics and no account of its own. As the provider of the app, I do not receive any data from you. Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud.

This is a translation of the German privacy policy. If the two differ, the German version applies.

Data on your device

Cook iT stores meals, drinks, the meal plan and the shopping list locally on your device. Cook iT does not run a server of its own for this.

Syncing with iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Cook iT syncs this data between your devices through Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit). It is kept in your personal iCloud storage, which I have no access to. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Sharing a household

If you invite other people with “Share household”, they see and edit the meals, the plan and the shopping list of that household. Sharing works through iCloud. Apple may show the participants details of the Apple Account, for example the name.

Recipe from image

You choose which photo or screenshot Cook iT receives. iOS asks for your permission before the camera is used. The analysis runs on your device with Apple’s text recognition and, where available, with the language model of Apple Intelligence. Cook iT does not upload your images anywhere.

Nutrition values

Cook iT calculates nutrition values on your device from a table that is included in the app. No data is transferred for this.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address. The GitHub General Privacy Statement applies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, the right to data portability and the right to object. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. You can delete your data in Cook iT yourself at any time by removing it in the app or by deleting the app. You manage your iCloud data in the iCloud settings of your device.
